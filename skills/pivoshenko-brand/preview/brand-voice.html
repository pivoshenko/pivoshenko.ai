Do And Don't
●No posts yet.
●Oops! Nothing here yet - check back soon!
●I keep my dotfiles, themes and skills in one place.
●We deliver world-class solutions that empower developers.
●Kasetto syncs this catalog into local agent configs.
●Kasetto seamlessly unlocks a game-changing agent workflow.
●Repository ↗
●Check out the awesome repo, it's insanely good!

First person singular. Never "we" - there is no company. Refuse hype (game-changing, cutting-edge), vague value (solutions, seamless, empower), and filler (simply, just, basically). If a sentence would survive on a SaaS landing page, rewrite it.

● smell test

A finished paragraph reads like a notebook entry rather than a pitch, survives being chopped in half with every sentence still standing alone, and loses nothing if every adjective is deleted.
