Principles

Color

Popil, warm ash, 26 slots. Build with role tokens, never raw slots.

The accent channel

Components read var(--accent). A site repoints it once and every accent place follows.

Type

JetBrains Mono sets everything by default, at weights 100 to 800.

The display face

Martian Mono is for page titles and large headers only - tight tracking, weight 700.

Pattern

One drifting field of lines per viewport, masked away from the copy, one variant per site.

Levels 2 to 4 indent. Anything deeper is noise - cut the list instead.
