// how to read these

Each card shows one component in the states its props allow, with the author's own content. The markup is the rendered output of the React component in the design system, so the class names and data attributes are the contract - copy them exactly. The artifact is the source of truth; when the two disagree, re-sync from it rather than editing here.

The things a page is made of: one item, many items, and the numbers over them.

components-card.html↗

One linked thing: a project, a catalog entry, a doc, a sibling site.

One number with its label - counts, totals, versions.

Nothing here yet, and what to do about it.
